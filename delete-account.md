# Delete your Ottr account

**App:** Ottr (Android)  ·  **Developer:** Ottr, United Arab Emirates (the
registered company's name replaces this once it exists)

## How to ask

1. Email **ottrsupport@gmail.com** **from the email address you use to sign in
   to Ottr**, with the subject **"Delete my account"**. Using that address is
   how we know the request is yours.
2. We confirm we have received it within 3 working days, and delete your
   account within **30 days**. We email you when it is done.

An in-app "Delete my account" button is coming in a later version.

## What is deleted

- Your Ottr account and sign-in email.
- Your cloud backup: every entry, category, budget and setting stored on our
  server.
- The daily counts of your requests to Otto and smart categories.

## What is not deleted, and why

- **Your votes on shop categories.** If you moved a purchase to a different
  category, your choice was kept as a vote on what kind of shop it is, stored
  under a code rather than your name or email. These votes are kept after your
  account is deleted: once the account is gone, the code can no longer be
  linked to you, so the votes point to no one, and they keep the shared shop
  list useful for everyone.
- **The shared list of shops** (shop names and kinds of shop). It holds nothing
  about you.
- **Data on your phone.** We cannot reach it. Delete the Ottr app, or use
  Settings → Clear history in the app, to remove it.
- **Otto conversations** were never stored on our server, so there is nothing
  to delete there. Our AI provider, Anthropic, deletes each request within 30
  days of receiving it, longer only if it is flagged for misuse.

## Delete some data without closing your account

- Delete any entry in the app; if backup is on, it is removed from the cloud
  too.
- Switch cloud backup off in Settings. Entries already backed up stay in the
  cloud until you delete your account; write to us if you want them removed
  sooner.
