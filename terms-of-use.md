## Check your numbers

Ottr's totals are only as good as the entries behind them. Automatic capture
can miss a transaction, read one wrongly or record it twice.

**Your bank statement is the authority, not Ottr.** Check Ottr's figures
against it before relying on them, and treat totals as a helpful estimate,
not an official record.
